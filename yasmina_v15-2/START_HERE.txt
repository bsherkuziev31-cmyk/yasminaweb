YASMINA COLLECTION — SIMPLE START

1. Double-click START_YASMINA.command
2. Your browser will open the Admin page automatically.
3. Log in with your admin account.
4. Go to Connections → Notifications.
5. Paste your Telegram bot token and click Save bot token.
6. In Telegram, open your bot and send /start.
7. Back in Yasmina, click Find chat ID.
8. Turn on Enable order notifications.
9. Click Save connection settings.
10. Click Send test message.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not send your Telegram bot token to anyone.
- Keep the Terminal window open while Yasmina is running.
- To stop Yasmina, close the Terminal window.
